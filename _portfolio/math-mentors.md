[GitHub repository](https://github.com/juholee10/math-mentors) · [math-mentors.org](https://math-mentors.org) · Main language: TypeScript

Math Mentors is a volunteer organization where students write problem sheets, packets, equation sheets, and full guides for math courses at any level, typeset in LaTeX. The guides are written and reviewed by students who have recently taken the course, so they focus on the parts that students actually find difficult. Everything is published online for free, with no paywall or account required.
