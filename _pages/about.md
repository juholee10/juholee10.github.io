I am a high school student at an international school in Seoul, South Korea. I am interested in applied mathematics and machine learning.

My current research looks at what happens to diversity when generative models are retrained on their own synthetic data. Through the Seoul Research Program, mentored by Professor Haifeng Xu (University of Chicago), I wrote "[Modeling Diversity Decay in Synthetic Retraining](/publication/2026-diversity-decay)," which is currently under review. This site collects my papers, projects, and essays.
