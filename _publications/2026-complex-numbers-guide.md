A full precalculus-level guide to complex numbers, written for Math Mentors for students who know basic algebra, trigonometry, and geometry. It covers the four operations, the complex plane, modulus and argument, polar form, De Moivre's Theorem, Euler's Formula, and finding complex roots, with worked examples and practice problems for each topic.
